# Independent scientific review packet — eight stored candidates

**Project:** Global Critical Care Guidelines Research Database (GCCGRD)  
**Review scope:** scientific eligibility and record verification for DOM-061–DOM-067 and DOM-070  
**Status:** awaiting independent reviewer  
**Prepared:** 2026-11-14

## Purpose and reviewer independence

This packet is for an independent second assessment of the eight candidates whose publisher-version PDFs are stored in `sources/open-access/`. The first-pass screening was research-assisted and is not a substitute for a qualified human review. The reviewer must be a person who did not conduct the first-pass assessment and was not involved in developing the guidelines being reviewed.

This is a **database eligibility verification**, not a clinical endorsement, treatment recommendation, or a full AGREE II methodological quality appraisal. The reviewer should verify primary-source facts, population and ICU relevance, document type, provenance, exact edition, access, licence, geography, methods, and status. Do not infer that a guideline is high quality merely because it passes catalogue inclusion.

## Required review questions for every candidate

1. Does the primary publisher/issuing-body source match the exact title, authors/working group, DOI, publication date and stored PDF version?
2. Is it an eligible formal guideline, consensus statement/definition, or formal position statement with clinical recommendations?
3. Is the issuing organisation an appropriate professional, governmental or international authority?
4. Does it directly address adult critical-care practice or critically ill adults?
5. If children are included, are adult recommendations clearly separable in the document? Identify exact pages/sections. If not, mark **Hold** or **Reject** under the project's adult-population rule.
6. Is the complete text publicly readable without payment or institutional login at the official source?
7. Are development methods (e.g. GRADE, Delphi, evidence-to-decision) explicitly reported? Record only what the document says.
8. Is the geographic classification supported by formal issuing/endorsing arrangements, rather than author affiliations alone?
9. Is the edition current, superseded, withdrawn, archived historical, or status unknown? Cite an issuing-body statement or version-history evidence.
10. Are companion publications, translations, errata, corrections and duplicate manifestations correctly linked rather than counted as separate guidelines?
11. Are licence and reuse restrictions accurately represented? Public access must not be equated with permission to redistribute.

## Candidate-specific issues requiring explicit attention

- **DOM-061:** mixed child/adult sepsis guidance. Confirm adult recommendations are clearly separable and assess the exact J Intensive Care English manifestation, not a parallel journal manifestation.
- **DOM-062:** confirm adult population and ICU rehabilitation scope, exact guideline status and official issuing-body provenance.
- **DOM-063:** verify adult ICU sepsis scope, resource-limited setting applicability and whether the position statement meets the formal document-type criterion.
- **DOM-064:** verify adult population and whether the recommendations are formal position-statement recommendations rather than narrative expert opinion alone.
- **DOM-065:** age scope is not clearly adult-only. Determine whether adult-specific recommendations are sufficiently separable; explicitly assess pregnancy and older-adult sections in context.
- **DOM-066:** includes adults and children. Determine whether adult recommendations are sufficiently separable, including adult dosing/management recommendations and pregnancy-specific recommendations.
- **DOM-067:** confirm adult scope/separability and whether the 41-question evidence/consensus format meets the project's document-type criterion.
- **DOM-070:** verify adult scope, formal issuing and endorsement relationships, and the relationship to SCCM/ASHP source guidance (including whether this is a distinct contextualised guideline).

## Decision per candidate

For each ID, choose one:
- **Eligible** — every mandatory criterion passes.
- **Reject** — one or more mandatory criteria fail.
- **Hold** — mandatory evidence remains unclear or unavailable.
- **Return for correction** — an exact metadata or source mismatch appears correctable.

For each pass/fail/unclear decision, provide the primary-source URL and exact page/section/table or quoted locator. A DOI or abstract alone is not sufficient evidence for population separability or recommendation content.

## Reviewer credentials and conflict declaration

Please provide:
- Name or professional identifier suitable for the public audit trail (or a public GitHub handle; private contact details should be shared privately, not in this issue).
- Current professional role and relevant adult critical-care expertise.
- Experience in guideline development, evidence synthesis, GRADE, Delphi/consensus methods or guideline appraisal.
- Confirmation of whether you authored, edited, endorsed, funded or otherwise participated in any of these eight documents or their issuing groups.
- Other relevant financial, professional or intellectual conflicts.
- Review date and expected completion date.

A relevant conflict does not always automatically disqualify a reviewer, but it must be declared and assessed before assignment. A reviewer involved in the development of a particular candidate must not independently review that candidate.
