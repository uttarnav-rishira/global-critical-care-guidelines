# National and regional coverage audit — 2000–2026

**Audit date:** 4 November 2026  
**Scope:** Adult critical-care guidance issued by national or regional authorities and professional societies.  
**Purpose:** Discovery and gap assessment only. This document does not claim exhaustive coverage.

## Australia and New Zealand

### Sources checked
- ANZICS clinical guidance catalogue: https://www.anzics.org/advocacy/clinical-guidance/
- ANZCOR guideline portal: https://www.anzcor.org/
- ANZICS historical end-of-life statement (2014): https://www.anzics.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/08/ANZICS-Statement-on-Care-and-Decision-Making-at-the-End-of-Life-for-the-Critically-Ill.pdf
- ANZICS annual reports and historical material.

### Candidate and findings
- **ANZICS Statement on Withholding and Withdrawing Treatment (2003):** The 2014 ANZICS statement explicitly identifies this as the first two-page edition. The original 2003 document has not yet been retrieved. It is recorded as a historical predecessor candidate only.
- **ANZICS Statement on Care and Decision-Making at the End of Life for the Critically Ill, Edition 1.0 (2014):** Official ANZICS PDF is publicly readable. The document provides an Australasian framework and recommendations. It is copyrighted; reproduction enquiries are directed to ANZICS. Adult scope and predecessor/successor status require reviewer confirmation.
- ANZCOR portal provides adult advanced life support and other resuscitation guidance. Its current portal is dynamically updated and does not, by itself, establish historical versions or publication dates. Each adult guideline edition must be traced through archived versions and primary publication records before adding.

### Coverage gaps
Search ANZICS, ANZCOR, CICM, ANZCA, NHMRC, state health departments and Australian Commission on Safety and Quality in Health Care separately. Prioritise adult ICU transport, sepsis, transfusion, blood management, organ donation/death determination, end-of-life care, ICU triage, ventilation, antimicrobial stewardship and critical-care service standards. Separate individual-patient CPGs from standards and professional position statements.

## Africa, Middle East and Latin America

Search recognised national critical-care societies and regional bodies, including the Critical Care Society of Southern Africa and relevant national societies in Latin America and the Middle East. A current society archive may contain only recent documents; use PubMed, regional journals and official PDF archives to identify older editions. Retain the issuing body's original language and record translation availability.

## Method for the next coverage pass

For each country/region and domain:
1. Search the issuing body's official archive by title, synonym and year range.
2. Search PubMed/Europe PMC and regional bibliographic indexes for the same topic and issuing body.
3. Search the society's journal for formal guidelines, consensus statements and position statements.
4. Record all candidates and exclusions in the screening register; preserve exact query, date, database, and result count.
5. Check full text in a logged-out session and record access and licence separately.
6. Search for corrections, withdrawals, reaffirmations and superseding editions.
7. Assign a second reviewer before promotion into `data/guidelines.csv`.

## Known limitations

- This is a source map and preliminary audit, not a systematic review with reproducible result counts.
- The absence of a candidate from this file does not mean that a country or society has no eligible guidance.
- The 2000–2026 target period ends on 31 December 2026; searches completed on 4 November 2026 do not cover the remainder of 2026.
- Public readability is not permission to reproduce or redistribute a PDF.
- Service standards, accreditation documents, local protocols and audit indicators should remain separate from the main clinical-practice-guideline catalogue unless the inclusion criteria are formally expanded.

## Supplemental regional discovery — 4 November 2026

This is a targeted supplement to the preliminary audit, not a systematic or exhaustive search. Official society catalogues and primary journal records were checked for additional national/regional sources and recently published guidance.

### Australia and New Zealand
- **ANZCOR:** the live portal contains adult advanced life support and other resuscitation guidance, with dates/versions that may change. Each adult guideline needs a separate dated record and archived-version check; do not treat the portal as a single publication. The portal itself warns that guidelines may be modified at any time and are current from the date of printing: https://www.anzcor.org/
- **CICM:** the Professional Documents catalogue contains clinical guidance, statements, transport guidance and service standards. Screen each document by type; many concern professional governance or service provision rather than patient-level CPG recommendations: https://cicm.org.au/Web/Web/Policy-Advocacy/Professional-Documents.aspx
- **Priority action:** extract individual adult ANZCOR ALS and special-circumstance guidelines; review CICM IC-10 (transport of critically ill patients), IC-14 (withholding/withdrawing treatment), IC-16 (telemedicine in ICU), and IC-22 (sedation for acute behavioural disturbance) for scope and eligibility.

### United Kingdom
- **Intensive Care Society guideline catalogue:** includes current and archived documents; the Society explicitly warns archived items may be out of date or superseded. Search the resource library and record each edition separately: https://ics.ac.uk/guidance.html
- **GPICS V3 (2026):** jointly published by FICM and ICS on 28 January 2026. This is a national adult ICU service standards framework (minimum standards and quality-service recommendations), not automatically a patient-level clinical practice guideline. Recommend a separate service-standards collection, retaining earlier 2015, 2019 and 2022 editions: https://ficm.ac.uk/gpics
- **Priority action:** retrieve ICS acute status epilepticus guidance, transfer of critically ill adults, delirium guidance, end-of-life transfer guidance, and archived documents. Classify standards separately from clinical CPGs.

### Scandinavia, Nordic region and Gulf states
- **SSAI guideline catalogue:** society clinical practice committee uses GRADE and lists ICU guidance, including a 2024 rapid practice guideline on ketamine analgo-sedation in mechanically ventilated adults: https://ssai.info/guidelines/
- **Saudi/Kuwait/Nordic contextualised guidance:** a 2026 stress-ulcer-prophylaxis CPG was jointly developed by the Saudi Critical Care Society and SSAI and endorsed by the Kuwait Anesthesia and Critical Care Society. It explicitly adapts recommendations to Saudi Arabia, Kuwait and Nordic settings; candidate DOM-070. A 2024 Saudi/SSAI ketamine rapid practice guideline is candidate DOM-071.
- **Priority action:** screen SSAI's full catalogue, Saudi Critical Care Society guideline chapter and Kuwait Anesthesia and Critical Care Society publications as separate sources. Do not infer geographic classification from author affiliations alone; use formal issuing/endorsing bodies and panel documentation.

### Japan
- **Japanese Society of Intensive Care Medicine (JSICM):** its English publication catalogue lists the Japanese sepsis guideline (2024 edition; English version 2025), J-ReCIP 2023 rehabilitation guideline, nutrition support guidance and ARDS guideline 2021: https://www.jsicm.org/en/publication/
- Candidate records DOM-061 and DOM-062 have been added for Japanese sepsis and critical-care rehabilitation guidance. The Japanese sepsis guideline includes paediatric content; verify adult recommendation separability before inclusion.
- **Priority action:** retrieve JSICM nutrition, ARDS and older sepsis guideline editions, and screen Japanese-language original documents where the English translation is incomplete or absent.

### India
- **Indian Society of Critical Care Medicine (ISCCM):** the 2024 supplement contains a cluster of ICU position statements on resource-limited sepsis, invasive fungal infections, severe dengue, severe malaria and tuberculosis. Candidates DOM-063 to DOM-067.
- **Priority action:** search ISCCM's full guideline/position-statement archive across 2000–2026, including ventilation, AKI/RRT, poisoning, obstetric critical care, nutrition and infection. Verify each article's formal adult population and development method; a society position statement qualifies only when it contains formal clinical recommendations.

### China
- The Chinese Society of Critical Care Medicine's 2025 guideline on construction and management of critical care medicine is a national service-development/organisation framework, not a patient-level clinical CPG. The English-language version is an authorised translation and publicly available; do not create a duplicate record for the Chinese original and English translation. Consider it for a separate service-standards collection, with language and translation metadata.
- **Priority action:** search the Chinese Society of Critical Care Medicine and Chinese Medical Association archives in Chinese as well as English. English-only searching will substantially undercount national guidance.

### Remaining geographic gaps
The present search still has limited primary-source coverage for:
- Sub-Saharan Africa and pan-African critical-care bodies;
- Latin America, particularly Brazil, Argentina, Chile, Colombia and regional intensive-care federations;
- Middle East beyond Saudi Arabia/Kuwait;
- South and Southeast Asia beyond India and Japan;
- China and other East Asian national-language publications;
- lower- and middle-income country guidance and resource-adapted CPGs.

For each region, search national critical-care societies, ministries of health, resuscitation councils, specialty societies and national guideline repositories. Record source language and whether a translation is official. Absence of an English result must not be interpreted as absence of a national guideline.

### Search evidence and verification limits
Sources checked for this supplement include official ANZCOR, CICM, ICS, FICM, SSAI and JSICM portals, plus primary journal/PubMed records for the 2024–2026 candidate guidelines. The new candidate register records the primary-source URLs and unresolved access/licence/scope questions. This supplement does not claim systematic database coverage, exhaustive retrieval, or independent second review.
