# National and regional coverage audit — 2000–2026

**Audit date:** 4 November 2026  
**Scope:** Adult critical-care guidance issued by national or regional authorities and professional societies.  
**Purpose:** Discovery and gap assessment only. This document does not claim exhaustive coverage.

## Australia and New Zealand

### Sources checked
- ANZICS clinical guidance catalogue: https://www.anzics.org/advocacy/clinical-guidance/
- ANZCOR guideline portal: https://www.anzcor.org/
- ANZICS historical end-of-life statement (2014): https://www.anzics.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/08/ANZICS-Statement-on-Care-and-Decision-Making-at-the-End-of-Life-for-the-Critically-Ill.pdf
- ANZICS annual reports and historical material.

### Candidate and findings
- **ANZICS Statement on Withholding and Withdrawing Treatment (2003):** The 2014 ANZICS statement explicitly identifies this as the first two-page edition. The original 2003 document has not yet been retrieved. It is recorded as a historical predecessor candidate only.
- **ANZICS Statement on Care and Decision-Making at the End of Life for the Critically Ill, Edition 1.0 (2014):** Official ANZICS PDF is publicly readable. The document provides an Australasian framework and recommendations. It is copyrighted; reproduction enquiries are directed to ANZICS. Adult scope and predecessor/successor status require reviewer confirmation.
- ANZCOR portal provides adult advanced life support and other resuscitation guidance. Its current portal is dynamically updated and does not, by itself, establish historical versions or publication dates. Each adult guideline edition must be traced through archived versions and primary publication records before adding.

### Coverage gaps
Search ANZICS, ANZCOR, CICM, ANZCA, NHMRC, state health departments and Australian Commission on Safety and Quality in Health Care separately. Prioritise adult ICU transport, sepsis, transfusion, blood management, organ donation/death determination, end-of-life care, ICU triage, ventilation, antimicrobial stewardship and critical-care service standards. Separate individual-patient CPGs from standards and professional position statements.

## Africa, Middle East and Latin America

Search recognised national critical-care societies and regional bodies, including the Critical Care Society of Southern Africa and relevant national societies in Latin America and the Middle East. A current society archive may contain only recent documents; use PubMed, regional journals and official PDF archives to identify older editions. Retain the issuing body's original language and record translation availability.

## Method for the next coverage pass

For each country/region and domain:
1. Search the issuing body's official archive by title, synonym and year range.
2. Search PubMed/Europe PMC and regional bibliographic indexes for the same topic and issuing body.
3. Search the society's journal for formal guidelines, consensus statements and position statements.
4. Record all candidates and exclusions in the screening register; preserve exact query, date, database, and result count.
5. Check full text in a logged-out session and record access and licence separately.
6. Search for corrections, withdrawals, reaffirmations and superseding editions.
7. Assign a second reviewer before promotion into `data/guidelines.csv`.

## Known limitations

- This is a source map and preliminary audit, not a systematic review with reproducible result counts.
- The absence of a candidate from this file does not mean that a country or society has no eligible guidance.
- The 2000–2026 target period ends on 31 December 2026; searches completed on 4 November 2026 do not cover the remainder of 2026.
- Public readability is not permission to reproduce or redistribute a PDF.
- Service standards, accreditation documents, local protocols and audit indicators should remain separate from the main clinical-practice-guideline catalogue unless the inclusion criteria are formally expanded.
