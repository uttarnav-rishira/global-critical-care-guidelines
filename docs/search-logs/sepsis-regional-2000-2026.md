## Search method and limitations

Targeted searches were conducted across official issuing-body catalogues and primary bibliographic/full-text sources using jurisdiction + sepsis guideline terms, with checks for adult scope, document type, publication date, DOI/PMID/PMCID, and publicly accessible full text. This was not a complete search of every country, language, or national society. Exact result counts were not captured by the search interface; do not report them as zero or estimate them.

## Follow-up actions

1. Obtain the full official WHO guideline PDF and identify whether adult recommendations are separately presented.
2. Download and screen the 2026 British Columbia adult guideline PDF; determine whether it is a formal guideline and whether methodology is documented.
3. Inspect the full German S3 2025 guideline for age scope, methodology, recommendation grading, and exact version date.
4. Review the Japanese J-SSCG2024 English version and supporting appendices; separate adult recommendations and record the original Japanese version date plus English publication date.
5. Screen NICE NG51/NG253 against the strict 18+ eligibility rule; if adult-specific extraction is impossible, exclude transparently.
6. Search additional countries and regions (e.g., France, Spain, Italy, Netherlands, Scandinavia, India, Brazil, South Africa, Singapore, Taiwan) using native-language search terms and official society repositories.
7. Keep national standards, implementation pathways, and local protocols in an exclusions/related-resources register rather than inflating the guideline count.

## Search completeness statement

**Preliminary targeted regional discovery only.** It is not an exhaustive global search. No claim is made that all national or regional sepsis guidelines from 2000–2026 have been identified.
