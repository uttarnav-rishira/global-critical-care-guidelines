# Sepsis and septic shock historical search log

**Work package:** GCCGRD-WP01  
**Search date:** 2026-11-03  
**Search coverage:** 2000-01-01 to 2026-11-03 (the database's planned end date is 2026-12-31; that future period cannot yet be searched).  
**Population:** Adults aged 18 years or older; mixed-population documents require separable adult recommendations.  
**Domain:** Sepsis, septic shock, sepsis definitions, and directly relevant international critical-care consensus.

## Purpose and limitations

This is the first-pass discovery log for the sepsis work package. It identifies candidate documents from primary publisher/issuing-body pages and bibliographic records. It is **not** a completed systematic review, exhaustive search, or final eligibility determination. Search-engine result counts were not consistently exposed in the discovery interface and are therefore recorded as **not captured**, rather than estimated. Searches must be rerun in PubMed and relevant society archives with exportable counts before claiming PRISMA-style completeness.

## Reproducible PubMed search strings

Run each query in PubMed and save the date, exact query, number retrieved, and exported PMID set in a dated search record. These are discovery searches, not final eligibility filters.

1. **SSC guidelines**
   `("Surviving Sepsis Campaign"[Title/Abstract] OR "surviving sepsis"[Title/Abstract]) AND (guideline*[Title/Abstract] OR recommendation*[Title/Abstract]) AND ("2000/01/01"[Date - Publication] : "2026/11/03"[Date - Publication])`

2. **International definitions / consensus**
   `(sepsis[Title/Abstract] AND (definition*[Title/Abstract] OR consensus[Title/Abstract] OR task force[Title/Abstract])) AND (international[Title/Abstract] OR "critical care"[Title/Abstract] OR intensive[Title/Abstract]) AND ("2000/01/01"[Date - Publication] : "2026/11/03"[Date - Publication])`

3. **Broader critical-care guidance**
   `(sepsis OR septic shock) AND (guideline OR consensus statement OR position statement OR practice recommendation) AND (adult OR adults OR critically ill OR intensive care)`

4. **Focused updates / corrections**
   `("Surviving Sepsis Campaign"[Title/Abstract] OR "sepsis and septic shock"[Title/Abstract]) AND (update OR correction OR erratum OR addendum OR revision)`

Do not use an adult title/abstract filter as a hard exclusion: adult population eligibility must be checked in the full document, because some older SSC papers include a paediatric subgroup alongside adult guidance.

## Important date and edition rules

- Record the **earliest official publication date** (including online-first date) where documented; retain the conventional edition label in the title/short title.
- The 2008 SSC article appeared online on 2007-12-04 and in the 2008 journal issue. Record both dates; do not silently treat the online-first date as the issue year.
- The 2012, 2016, 2021, and 2026 SSC editions have parallel publications in more than one journal. These are not automatically separate guideline editions. Select one canonical record per edition and store the parallel DOI/citation in notes or a future related-publications table.
- The 2021 SSC guideline's PMC page describes a limited reuse permission linked to the WHO COVID-19 declaration. Readability and reuse rights are separate fields.
- The 2026 SSC guideline is the latest candidate identified at this search date. Do not describe the historical search as complete through 2026-12-31 until that date has passed and the search is rerun.
