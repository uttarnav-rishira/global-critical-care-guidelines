## Promotion rule

A candidate may be promoted to the public catalogue only when:
1. Reviewer 1 and Reviewer 2 independently pass all mandatory eligibility checks;
2. any disagreement is resolved and documented;
3. full-text access and exact edition are confirmed;
4. licence/reuse terms are recorded accurately;
5. the public record contains a complete audit trail and linked predecessor/successor records where relevant.

If the full text is public but reuse permission is restricted or unclear, the catalogue may link to the source and describe its access status; it must not reproduce the document. If any mandatory eligibility criterion is unclear, retain the record in the screening register.
