# GCCGRD domain expansion — work programme

**Project:** Global Critical Care Guidelines Research Database (GCCGRD)  
**Reference date:** 2026-11-03  
**Scope:** Historical adult critical-care guidelines, formal consensus statements/definitions and eligible evidence-based position statements from 2000 onward. Planned coverage end date: 2026-12-31; searches before that date cannot establish completeness for the remainder of 2026.

## Purpose

This programme replicates the sepsis work-package workflow across the remaining 15 controlled clinical domains. It creates reproducible search protocols and screening infrastructure first. Candidate discovery is not equivalent to eligibility or verification, and no candidate is promoted to `data/guidelines.csv` until all inclusion criteria, public full-text access, licence/access terms, identifiers, edition status and independent review have been checked.

## Common workflow

1. Search issuing-body archives and bibliographic databases with domain-specific queries; retain exact search date and query.
2. Search by both document topic and formal document type. Do not hard-filter adult population from title/abstract; inspect the complete document.
3. Screen title, date, issuing authority, direct ICU relevance and formal document type.
4. Check the primary issuing-body or publisher document; resolve DOI/PMID/PMCID and record exact publication/online-first dates.
5. Test complete full-text access without payment/login. Record licence separately; publicly readable does not mean permission to republish.
6. Preserve each material edition/update as its own record; link parallel journal publications rather than duplicating the edition.
7. Check predecessor/successor, correction, withdrawal and current status against the issuing body. Do not infer supersession.
8. Obtain independent second review before promotion to the master database.
9. Record exclusions with reason; retain standards, toolkits and local pathways separately where useful but not eligible as formal CPGs.
10. Do not claim exhaustive coverage until search exports, deduplication, screening counts and country-by-country gap checks are complete.

## Initial candidate discoveries from the first web pass

The following are examples for targeted follow-up, not verified master records:

- **Airway:** Difficult Airway Society 2025 guideline for unanticipated difficult tracheal intubation in adults — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41203471/), [publisher DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bja.2025.10.006).
- **ARDS:** ATS 2024 update on management of adult ARDS — [PMC full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10870893/), DOI 10.1164/rccm.202311-2011ST; companion 2017 ATS/ESICM/SCCM guideline must be searched as a distinct historical edition.
- **Haemodynamics:** ESICM updated guideline on circulatory shock and haemodynamic monitoring in adult ICU patients — [ESICM announcement](https://www.esicm.org/esicm-guideline-circulatory-shock-haemodynamic-monitoring/); inspect linked primary publication for date, DOI and full text.
- **Cardiac:** ERC 2025 resuscitation guidelines — [ERC official guideline portal](https://www.erc.edu/science-research/guidelines/guidelines-2025/guidelines-2025-english/); [Resuscitation Council UK post-resuscitation summary](https://www.resus.org.uk/professional-library/2025-resuscitation-guidelines/post-resuscitation-care-guidelines).
- **Neurocritical:** Neurocritical Care Society guideline catalogue — [official catalogue](https://www.neurocriticalcare.org/Resources-Publications/Neurocritical-Care-Guidelines); individual adult guidelines and full-text access require document-level screening.
- **Sedation/analgesia/delirium:** SCCM 2025 focused PADIS update — [SCCM](https://www.sccm.org/clinical-resources/guidelines/guidelines/focused-update-padis-guideline), [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39982143/); preserve the 2018 PADIS guideline separately.
- **Rehabilitation:** ASSOBRAFIR 2026 ICU physical therapy guideline for critically ill adults — [PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13572998/), PMID 42710394. Verify publication date and licence.
- **Obstetric:** SMFM Consult Series #67, maternal sepsis (2023; reaffirmed 2025) — [SMFM official page](https://publications.smfm.org/publications/507-society-for-maternal-fetal-medicine-consult-series-67/), DOI 10.1016/j.ajog.2023.05.019. The population is pregnant/postpartum patients; use the obstetric domain and document overlap with sepsis.
- **Ethics/end-of-life:** SCCM 2025 clinical practice guidelines on adult end-of-life care in the ICU — [SCCM](https://www.sccm.org/clinical-resources/guidelines/guidelines/clinical-practice-guidelines-on-adult-end-of-life-care-in-the-icu), [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41342842/), DOI 10.1097/CCM.0000000000006856. The 2008 ACCM consensus statement is a historical predecessor candidate.

Other domains have search protocols and authoritative source targets but require further focused discovery before candidate titles are populated. This is intentional; no citations or candidate titles are invented to fill a quota.

## Quality safeguards

- The sepsis work package remains separate and unchanged as WP01.
- This programme is discovery infrastructure, not a claim that every domain has already been searched exhaustively.
- Adult age threshold remains 18+ unless the database's inclusion criteria explicitly allow a separately extractable adult subgroup.
- Paediatric, neonatal and obstetric populations are not automatically included in the general adult domain; obstetric critical care has its own domain.
- Public full-text availability and redistribution licence are separate.
- GCCGRD is a research index, not clinical decision support.
