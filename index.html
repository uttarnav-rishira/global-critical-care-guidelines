How records are assessed

Each edition is retained as a separate record. The database distinguishes discovery from verification so that provisional records are not mistaken for fully reviewed evidence.

  • Candidate identified: a potentially eligible document has been located.
  • Primary source checked: issuing-body or primary publication details have been checked.
  • Open-access verified: the complete text and access conditions have been checked.
  • Second review complete: an independent reviewer has checked the record.
